# Privacy Policy

**Mobile Heat Safety Tracker**

_Last updated: September 6, 2026_

## Overview

The Mobile Heat Safety Tracker ("the App") is designed to help coaches and athletic staff record heat safety checklists for sports practices. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used and stored.

## Information We Collect

The App collects and stores the following information that you enter directly into it:

- **Team information** — team names you create.
- **Practice information** — practice location, date, and head coach name.
- **Checklist entries** — observation time, air temperature, relative humidity, the calculated heat index, and the action taken (e.g., water break frequency, practice modification).

This information may include personal data such as the names of coaches or staff members you choose to record.

## How Information Is Stored

**All data is stored locally on your device.** The App does not transmit, upload, or sync your data to any remote server, cloud service, or third party. There is no account system, no login, and no backend database.

Specifically, data is stored using the device's local storage (AsyncStorage). Data remains on the device unless you:

- Export it to a file (CSV or PDF) using the App's export feature and share it yourself, or
- Delete the App or clear the App's data through your device settings.

## How Information Is Used

Your data is used solely to provide the App's core functionality:

- Recording and displaying heat safety checklists.
- Calculating the National Weather Service heat index.
- Color-coding entries according to heat index thresholds.
- Generating CSV/PDF exports you explicitly request.

## Data Sharing

The App does **not** share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to any third party. The App does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking services.

## Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services. Diagnostic logs (e.g., data-loading errors) are written only to the local device for troubleshooting and are not transmitted anywhere.

## Advertising

The App does not display advertising and does not collect data for advertising purposes.

## Permissions

The App requests only the permissions needed for its features:

- **Storage / file access** — to export checklists as CSV or PDF files (via the document picker and sharing features).
- **Sharing** — to let you intentionally share exported files with other apps on your device.

The App does not request or access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or other unrelated device features.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by coaches and athletic staff and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Choices and Controls

Because your data is stored only on your device, you have full control over it:

- **View and edit** your data at any time within the App.
- **Export** your data as CSV or PDF whenever you wish.
- **Delete** individual entries, teams, or practices, or clear all data through the App.
- **Remove** all data entirely by deleting the App or clearing its storage in your device settings.

## Data Retention

Data remains on your device for as long as you keep it. We do not retain any copy of your data on any server. Once you delete the App or clear its data, your information is removed.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Material changes may be highlighted within the App where appropriate.

## Contact

We do not offer a contact channel for this App. Because all data remains on your own device, no support or account management is required from us.
